## 4. Unauthorised Access Rules

RULE-ACCESS-01: Any entry by an unauthorised role MUST be logged as OccupancyEvent::UnauthorisedAccess with person_id, zone, and timestamp.

RULE-ACCESS-02: Security personnel MUST be notified within 2 minutes of detection.

RULE-ACCESS-03: The KG node MUST include authorized=NO and a reference to this rule. RULE-ACCESS-04: Chemical Storage has zero-tolerance policy. Any UNAUTHORIZED_ACCESS triggers immediate escalation to the Safety Officer regardless of role.
