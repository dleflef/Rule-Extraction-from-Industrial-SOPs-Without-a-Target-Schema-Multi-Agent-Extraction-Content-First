Effective: 2026-05-15
Middleton Digester Station - GreenField Biogas Solutions, LLC

1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This procedure defines normal operating setpoints, automated control responses,
maintenance triggers, and safety restrictions for the anaerobic digestion and
biogas utilisation systems at the Middleton facility. All operations, control
room, and maintenance personnel shall adhere to these limits and response
requirements.

3.0 AUTOMATED CONTROL AND ALARM RESPONSE RULES

3.1 Biogas Quality Interlock
If the methane concentration (CH4) measured by the biogas analyser GAS-701 falls
below 50% vol for a period exceeding 30 minutes, the DCS shall stop the CHP-1001
engine and divert all biogas to the flare FLR-601. A "Low CH4" Warning alarm is
raised. If CH4 drops below 40% vol, the alarm escalates to Critical and the plant
siren activates.

3.2 Agitator Interlock
The digester agitator AGT-301 shall not start unless the digester level is above
the minimum agitator immersion level (2.0 m from tank bottom). If the level falls
below 2.0 m, the DCS stops the agitator immediately.

3.3 H2S Alarm Acknowledgment
Whenever the High H2S alarm (H2S-901 outlet >150 ppm) is active, the control room
operator must acknowledge it within 5 minutes. If the alarm is not acknowledged
within 5 minutes, the DCS automatically initiates flaring and sends a plant-wide
alert notification.

3.4 Critical Alarm Acknowledgment Deadline
All alarms designated as "Critical" in the alarm list (including DGT-101/102 pH
Low-Low, BLR-501 High-High Steam Pressure, GSH-401 Level High-High, and CH4
<40% vol) must be acknowledged by the control room operator within 3 minutes of
annunciation. Failure to acknowledge escalates to the Shift Supervisor's pager
and activates the facility siren.

4.0 PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE AND DRIFT TRIGGERS

4.1 Agitator Vibration
If the RMS vibration of AGT-301, measured by sensor VIB-301, exceeds 7.1 mm/s
for more than 15 minutes continuously, a maintenance work order shall be
auto-generated with priority "Urgent", to be addressed within 24 hours.

4.2 Digester Temperature Probe Drift
The temperature of DGT-101 shall be measured by two independent probes (TT-101A
and TT-101B). If the absolute difference between the two readings exceeds 1.5C
for more than 1 hour, both probes must be inspected and recalibrated within
48 hours. A "Probe Drift" notification is logged.

4.3 Gas Analyser Calibration Drift
During daily auto-zero/span checks, if the GAS-701 zero drift exceeds 2% of full
scale (full scale = 100% vol CH4) or the span drift exceeds 5% of the reading,
the DCS shall schedule a recalibration task due within 48 hours and display an
"Analyzer Cal Required" message.

4.4 H2S Scrubber Media Replacement
When the differential pressure across the H2S scrubber H2S-901 exceeds 25 mbar,
the DCS shall generate a maintenance request to replace the media. The scrubber
may remain in service, but the alert indicates imminent bypass.

4.5 Boiler Service Interval
The steam boiler BLR-501 shall undergo a comprehensive inspection every 8,000
operating hours or 12 calendar months, whichever occurs first. The DCS shall
issue a "Boiler Service Due" reminder 30 days before the deadline.

4.6 Flare Ignition Test
The flare ignition system shall be tested weekly (every 168 hours). The test and
result (success/failure) must be logged in the shift log by the maintenance
technician.

5.0 ACCESS AND SAFETY RESTRICTIONS

5.1 Confined Space Entry - Digester Tanks
Entry into DGT-101 or DGT-102 is classified as confined space. A permit is
mandatory. Before entry, the atmosphere must be tested for CH4 (<10% LEL),
H2S (<10 ppm), and O2 (19.5-23.5%) within 15 minutes prior to entry. A
dedicated standby attendant with continuous radio contact shall be posted. The
entry supervisor must log the entry permit in the electronic system and obtain
acknowledgment from the Shift Supervisor within 15 minutes of permit activation.
If, during entry, the CH4 concentration exceeds 5% LEL, all personnel must
evacuate immediately and the evacuation alarm sounded.

5.2 Gas Holder Platform Access
Personnel are prohibited from accessing the GSH-401 maintenance platform when the
gas holder level exceeds 80%. A warning sign interlocked with the level
transmitter shall illuminate at the access gate.

5.3 Flare Area Exclusion
When the flare FLR-601 is in operation (flame detected), a 15-metre radius
exclusion zone is enforced. No personnel entry is permitted without explicit
authorisation from the Shift Supervisor.
