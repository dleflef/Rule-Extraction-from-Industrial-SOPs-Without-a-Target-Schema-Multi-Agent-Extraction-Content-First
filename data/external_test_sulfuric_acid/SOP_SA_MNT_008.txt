## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines the mandatory maintenance schedules, lockout/tagout (LOTO) requirements, and calibration intervals for SAP2 equipment.

## 2. Lockout/Tagout Procedure
2.1 Prior to any maintenance work, the equipment must be isolated from all hazardous energy sources and locked out in accordance with Apex LOTO Standard STD-SAF-012.
2.2 The Maintenance Supervisor shall verify zero energy state and issue a safety work permit before work commences.

## 3. Scheduled Maintenance Intervals
Table 3 lists minimum maintenance activities.

| Equipment | Maintenance Requirement |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Sulfur melter agitator M-101 | Inspect and grease bearings and check seal integrity every 2,000 operating hours; performed by Maintenance Technician. |
| Combustion air blower B-103 | Perform vibration analysis and bearing temperature check, replace oil every 4,000 operating hours; performed by Condition Monitoring Technician. |
| Acid circulation pump P-107A | Overhaul (replace mechanical seal, impeller wear rings) every 8,000 operating hours or 12 months, whichever first; performed by Maintenance Fitter. |
| Converter R-104 | Internal inspection and catalyst sampling every 4 years; performed by Inspection Engineer. |
| Absorption tower T-106 | Check packing and acid distributor, clean every 2 years; performed by Process Engineer. |

## 4. Instrument Calibration Schedule
Table 4 lists mandatory calibrations.

| Instrument | Calibration Requirement |
|------------|--------------------------|
| SO₂ analyzer AI-201 | Calibrate every 7 days using span gas at 10.0% SO₂; performed by Instrument Technician. |
| Temperature transmitters TI-201 through TI-206 | Calibrate every 3 months by 3-point check vs certified RTD; performed by Instrument Technician. |
| Differential pressure transmitters ΔP-201 to ΔP-204 | Calibrate every 6 months using deadweight tester; performed by Instrument Technician. |
| Flowmeter FI-401 | Verify calibration every 6 months by clamp-on ultrasonic method; performed by Instrument Technician. |

## 5. Permit Requirements
5.1 All hot work within 15 m of the sulfur melter requires a hot work permit and continuous gas monitoring for SO₂ and H₂S.
5.2 Confined space entry into the converter vessel or absorption tower requires a confined space permit, atmospheric testing, and a standby person.
