## 1. Purpose & Scope
This document defines the access rights, operational limits, and mandatory constraints for all personnel interacting with equipment inside WLCSP Cleanroom Bay 7 (Class 1000). It applies to Operators, Process Engineers, Maintenance Technicians, and QA Inspectors.

## 3. Automated Environmental Alarm Response Rules
The Bay Management System (BMS) shall execute the following actions without manual intervention.

1. **Humidity High**: If relative humidity in Bay 7 exceeds 45% RH for >5 continuous minutes, trigger a Yellow Alarm, reduce fresh air damper to 30% open, and notify Shift Lead.
2. **Humidity Critical**: If RH >55% for >2 minutes, trigger Red Alarm, shut down PC‑03 plasma gas supply, and evacuate bay within 90 seconds.
3. **Temperature Drift**: If average zone temperature deviates from 21.0 °C by ±1.5 °C for >10 minutes, adjust chilled water valve to compensate and log event; if deviation persists >30 min, escalate to Amber Alarm.
4. **Airborne Particle Spike**: If 0.5 µm particle count exceeds 1000 particles/m³ on any sensor for >3 consecutive minutes, disable AOI‑04 conveyor and hold current lot for QA review.
5. **Differential Pressure Loss**: If bay-to-gowning pressure falls below 15 Pa for >10 sec, close all bay doors, sound audible alert, and switch air handlers to recirculation mode.
6. **Exhaust Duct Failure**: If exhaust flow from PC‑03 drops below 80 SCFM for >5 sec, immediately cut power to plasma generator and close process gas valves.
7. **E‑Stop Activation**: When any emergency stop button is pressed, all equipment in the row de‑energises within 1 second; BMS logs the station ID and notifies Safety Officer via pager.
