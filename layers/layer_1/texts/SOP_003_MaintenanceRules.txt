## Predictive & Corrective Maintenance Rules - Production Line A

Document: SOP-003 | Rev: 1.5 | Date: 2025-09-01 | Author: Ing. A. Conti - Maintenance Engineering

## 1. Predictive Maintenance Rules

| Rule ID | Sens or | Station | Trigger | Action | Priority |
| ----------- | ----------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| MAINT-01 | VIB | ST04_PACKAGING | VIB DRIFT >0.05 mm/s over 60 min | Inspect and lubricate bearing | HIGH |
| MAINT-02 | CUR | ST02_SEALING | CUR DRIFT >1.0 A for 30+ min | Inspect sealing jaw | HIGH |
| MAINT-03 | TEN | ST03_LABELLING | TEN STUCK >10 samples | Replace sensor | MEDIUM |
| MAINT-04 | PRS | ST01_FILLING | PRS <3.3 bar for >15 min | Inspect filter and pump | MEDIUM |
| MAINT-05 | TMP | SRV01_SERVERRO OM | TMP >25°C for >5 min | Activate secondary cooling | CRITICAL |
| MAINT-06 | TMP | WRH01_WAREHOU SE | TMP DRIFT >2°C over 2 h | Inspect compressor; notify QC | CRITICAL |
| MAINT-07 | TMP | CHM01_CHEMICALS TORAGE | TMP >20°C for >10 min | Emergency ventilation; notify safety officer | CRITICAL |
| MAINT-08 | HUM | RND01_RDLAB | HUM DRIFT >10%RH over 60 min | Activate dehumidifier; check HVAC drain | HIGH |

## 2. Correlated Fault Resolution

| Pattern | Primary Fault | Secondary Effect | Resolution |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ST02_SEALING-CUR › → ST04_PACKAGING- SPD ↓ | Sealing motor overload | Conveyor speed drop via electrical coupling | 1.Verify ST02-CUR trend. 2.Inspect sealing 3.Reset ST04 speed setpoint. |
| SRV01-TMP › → SCADA latency › | Server Room overheating | Increased scan time → apparent STUCK readings | 1.Reduce SRV01-TMP <25°C. 2.Verify PLC heartbeats. 3.Clear STUCK alarms. |
| WRH01-TMP › → QC HOLD | Cold warehouse drift | Product quality risk | 1.Restore WRH01-TMP <8°C. 2.Notify QC. 3.Place batch on HOLD in ERP. |
